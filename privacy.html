Privacy Policy — LXL Driver

Lubana Xpress LLC — Last updated: November 13, 2026

LXL Driver is the company app for drivers and dispatchers at Lubana Xpress LLC ("LXL", "we", "us"), a trucking company based in Indianapolis, Indiana. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what we do with it. It applies to the LXL Driver app on iOS and Android, and to the same data when you use the app on a tablet or in the admin (dispatch) view.

If you have questions about this policy, contact Lubana Xpress LLC through the app (Contact Dispatch) or ask your dispatcher directly.

1. Who this app is for

LXL Driver is for LXL company drivers and dispatch staff only. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app is not offered to anyone under 18.

2. Account information

When dispatch creates your account, or when you sign in, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your emergency contact, if you provide one
  • Your profile photo, if you choose to upload one

We use this to identify you, to let dispatch assign you trucks, trailers and loads, to message you about work, and to contact you or your emergency contact if something goes wrong on the road. Your password is stored only as a secure hash — nobody at LXL can see your password.

3. Location

This is the most important part of this policy, so please read it carefully.

What we collect. While you are signed in to the app, we collect your device's location (latitude and longitude) and the time it was recorded — including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. This is how dispatch can see where the truck is while you are on a load.

Why. Dispatch needs your live location to assign and track loads, to check whether a truck is in the yard or on the road, to plan pickups and deliveries, and to send help if you break down or have an emergency. Location is a core part of this app — it is not used for advertising, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers.

When it stops. Location sharing starts when you sign in and stops when you sign out. If you sign out, close your account, or uninstall the app, we stop collecting your location. Turning off location permission on your phone also stops it, but dispatch will no longer be able to see where the truck is and some features (live location on the dispatch map) will not work.

Who sees it. Your live location is visible only to LXL dispatch staff (admins) inside the app, on the Locations screen. It is not shown to other drivers and it is not public.

On iPhone, the app asks for "Always" location permission so it can keep sharing your location with dispatch while you are signed in, even in the background. On Android, the app asks for background location permission for the same reason. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings, but the app will ask you to turn it back on while you are signed in and working.

4. Photos and documents

The app lets you take or choose photos of:

  • Bills of lading (BOLs) — pickup and delivery
  • Fuel receipts, toll receipts and other expense receipts
  • Driver documents and truck/trailer paperwork (registration, insurance, inspection, title, permits)

These photos and files are uploaded to LXL's server and stored as company records. Dispatch and company admins can view them to confirm deliveries, process reimbursements and advances, and keep the truck and trailer paperwork up to date. We keep them for as long as the company needs them for its business, tax and legal records.

5. Messages and requests

Messages you send to dispatch, and requests you make in the app (advances, time off, reimbursements, truck problems, unit changes), are stored so dispatch can read and answer them, and so there is a record of what was asked and decided. Admins can see your messages and requests; other drivers cannot.

6. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we send you push notifications about your work: new load assignments, dispatch messages, and decisions on your requests (approved or denied). Notifications are sent through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) via Expo's push service, which receives your device's push token and the notification text. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, but you may then miss load assignments and dispatch messages.

7. What we do NOT collect or share

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment card details in the app.
  • We do not collect your location when you are signed out.

The only outside services that receive any of your data are the ones needed to run the app: our own LXL server (which stores your account, loads, documents and locations), Apple or Google push services (to deliver notifications), and Expo's push service (which routes those notifications). Nobody else receives your information.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, load, document and location records for as long as you work for LXL and for as long as the company needs them afterwards for its business, tax and legal records.

To delete your data: contact dispatch through the app or speak to your dispatcher and ask for your account and personal data to be deleted. We will delete your account, profile photo, messages and location history. Some records we must keep by law (for example, signed delivery paperwork and tax records) may be kept in the company's business files even after your account is deleted, and we will tell you if that applies.

If you uninstall the app without signing out, your account still exists on our server — uninstalling does not delete your data. Ask dispatch to delete it, as above.

9. Data security

Your data is sent to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access is limited to you and to LXL dispatch staff. Your sign-in session is protected by a secure token stored on your device. If you believe someone else has used your account, tell dispatch immediately so we can sign you out and reset your password.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Contact dispatch to make any of these requests. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version in the app and on our store listings. If a change affects how we use your location or your personal information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
